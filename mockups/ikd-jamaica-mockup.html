Our story
Shotokan in Jamaica since 1976

Jamaica's connection to Shotokan karate runs deep. Shuseki Shihan Frank Woon-A-Tai — the IKD's founder — served as Jamaica's chief instructor from 1976 to 1980, laying the foundation for a tradition that endures today.

The Jamaica Shotokan Karate School, operating as IKD Jamaica, was established as a non-profit organisation in 2006 with a clear mission: to make karate accessible to everyone on the island, regardless of background or ability.

In 2016, Jamaica hosted the IKD Caribbean Cup in Kingston — a milestone event that showcased Jamaica's standing within the global IKD community and brought the Caribbean karate family together on Jamaican soil.

Why we train
Mission statement

"The IKD will use traditional and modern applied research to offer the highest level of karate to all students. IKD will uphold standards of excellence that inspire students to improve character development and instil core values of respect, honour and discipline — and encourage positive behaviour and empower children and youth to live socially and emotionally healthy lives."

Discipline
Training builds mental and physical discipline that carries beyond the dojo into everyday life and the wider community.
Respect
Mutual respect between students, instructors and opponents is the foundation of all IKD practice and tradition.
Community
Karate as a vehicle for youth development — keeping young people on a path of health, purpose and positive contribution.

Tournament formats
IKD competitive events
Individual kata
Solo performance of a prescribed kata sequence, judged on technique, power, speed, timing and focus.
Individual kumite
One-on-one controlled sparring. IKD rules emphasise budo principles — technique quality over aggression.
Team kata
Three-person team performing kata in perfect synchronisation. Precision and months of coordinated practice required.
Team kumite
Team-based sparring competition. Jamaica has reached the podium in this format at IKD World Cup events.
Bunkai
Practical application of kata techniques — performed with a partner to demonstrate the real-world meaning of each movement.
Enbu
Choreographed partner demonstration combining kata, bunkai and artistic expression. Unique to IKD competitive format.
